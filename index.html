From sample to countertop

Most kitchens take a few days from prep to full cure.

  1. Pick a board

    Choose a style from the samples. We can shift colors to match your cabinets or floors.

  2. Free estimate

    We come out, measure, and check what your counters are made of so we know the right prep.

  3. Prep and pour

    We cover your kitchen, prep the old surface, then pour and work the colors by hand.

  4. Let it cure

    Hands off while it hardens. We tell you when it is ready for light use and when it is fully cured.
